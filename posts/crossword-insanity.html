Introducing Crossword Insanity

We're excited to introduce Crossword Insanity, a crossword puzzle game from FocusFall.

Crossword Insanity is built around a simple idea: solving word puzzles should be easy to pick up, but challenging enough to keep you thinking.

Game: Crossword Insanity

Genre: Word Puzzle

Platform: Android

Developer: FocusFall

How It Works

Each level presents a crossword puzzle with a set of words to discover. Use the clues and available letters to fill the puzzle and complete the level.

The puzzles gradually become more challenging as you progress, giving you a reason to keep playing and improve your word-solving skills.

Designed for Quick Sessions

Crossword Insanity is designed to be played whenever you have a few spare minutes.

You don't need to commit to a long gaming session. Pick up the game, solve a few puzzles, and come back whenever you want.

Growing Difficulty

The game starts with simpler puzzles and gradually introduces more challenging words and combinations.

The goal is to provide a steady sense of progression without making the game unnecessarily complicated.

Built by FocusFall

Crossword Insanity is developed by FocusFall, an independent one-person game studio.

The game is part of our goal to create lightweight, accessible games with simple mechanics and satisfying gameplay.

What's Next?

Development doesn't stop when the game is released. We plan to continue improving Crossword Insanity and exploring new ideas for future updates and games.

We'll share development news and updates here on the FocusFall blog.
