About FocusFall

FocusFall is an independent game studio creating simple, engaging, and satisfying games.

Who We Are

FocusFall is an independent one-person game studio focused on creating mobile games with simple ideas and enjoyable gameplay.

We believe games don't always need complicated systems or huge worlds to be fun. A well-designed mechanic, a clear goal, and a satisfying challenge can be enough.

Our Games

CROSSWORD INSANITY

A word puzzle game where players solve crossword puzzles, discover words, and challenge themselves across increasingly difficult levels.

Our Approach

FocusFall focuses on creating games that are easy to pick up but rewarding to master.

We aim to keep our games accessible, lightweight, and enjoyable for players who want something they can play whenever they have a few minutes.
